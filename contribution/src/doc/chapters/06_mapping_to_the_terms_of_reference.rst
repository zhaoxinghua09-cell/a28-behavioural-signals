Mapping to the Terms of Reference
=================================

- §3.4 (security assessment criteria and benchmarks): the sixteen modes are candidate negative controls
  for any benchmark that scores an agentic system's self-reported checks.
- A.2.8 (behavioural trust signals): the split in section 3 states which signals can be inferred from
  artifacts and which require execution records, which is the question A.2.8 needs answered before
  signals are aggregated.
- §4.5 (guidelines, metrics and recommendations): a metric that counts "checks passed" without counting
  "checks that could have failed" measures reporting, not behaviour.
