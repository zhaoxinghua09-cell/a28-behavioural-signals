What A.2.8 asks, and what this note answers
===========================================

A.2.8 asks the group to consider behavioural signals as a basis for trust decisions: what an agent did, under
what constraints, and whether that record supports the conclusion drawn from it. This note takes one narrow
part of that question — whether the signal was produced by a check that could have failed — and answers it
with a catalogue rather than a framework.

No new terminology is introduced. "Silent failure" is used in its ordinary sense: the process completes,
the report says the check passed, and no check took place.
