Request to the Focus Group
==========================

#. Consider the sixteen modes as candidate negative controls in whatever conformance criteria the group
   adopts.
#. Consider recording, for each behavioural signal, whether the producing check was capable of returning
   failure — one field, not a new framework.
#. Treat text-level detection as a triage step only; do not accept it as evidence for the eight modes
   listed in section 3.
