Limits, and what is not claimed
===============================

The catalogue is empirical and incomplete; sixteen modes were collected from practice, not derived
systematically, and a seventeenth will appear. The control run covers one scanner and one corpus on one
date, and the corpus is small. Nothing here is a certification method, and nothing here is a claim that
a system passing these controls is trustworthy — it is a claim that a system failing them is not
evidencing anything.
